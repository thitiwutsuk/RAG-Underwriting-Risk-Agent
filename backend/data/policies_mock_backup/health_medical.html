SecureFuture Health & Medical Insurance Policy

Policy Series: HM-400 | Product Type: Individual Health and Hospitalization Insurance

1. Overview

This policy reimburses eligible medical expenses arising from covered illness or injury, including hospitalization, surgery, and outpatient treatment, up to the annual and per-condition limits stated in the policy schedule.

2. Eligibility

Applicants must be between 1 and 70 years of age at enrollment (dependents from birth). Applicants classified under the "Decline" underwriting tier for a specific condition may still be issued a policy with a permanent exclusion rider for that condition.

3. Coverage Details

  • In-patient hospitalization: room and board, up to the plan's daily room limit.
  • Surgical fees: as per the surgical schedule of benefits.
  • Outpatient treatment: consultations, diagnostic tests, and prescribed medication, subject to an annual outpatient limit.
  • Emergency accident treatment: covered from day one, with no waiting period.

4. Waiting Periods

  • General illness: 30 days from the policy effective date.
  • Specified illnesses (e.g., hernia, tonsillectomy, cataract, benign tumors): 120 days.
  • Pre-existing conditions disclosed at application: covered after 12 months of continuous coverage, subject to underwriting review.

5. Exclusions

  • Cosmetic or elective procedures not medically necessary.
  • Treatment for conditions arising from self-inflicted injury or substance abuse.
  • Experimental or unproven treatments not approved by the relevant medical authority.
  • Routine dental care and vision correction, unless covered by an optional rider.

6. Co-payment and Deductibles

Standard plans carry a 20% co-payment on outpatient claims after the annual deductible has been met. Applicants in the "Substandard" or "High Risk" underwriting tier may be offered coverage with an increased deductible or co-payment in lieu of a declined application.

7. Claims Process

For cashless hospitalization at a network provider, pre-authorization must be obtained at least 48 hours before a planned admission, or within 24 hours for emergency admission. Reimbursement claims for non-network providers must be submitted with itemized medical bills within 30 days of treatment.

8. Definitions

Network Provider: A hospital or clinic that has a direct billing arrangement with the insurer.

Annual Limit: The maximum total benefit payable within a single policy year.
