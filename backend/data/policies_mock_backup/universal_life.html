SecureFuture Universal Life Insurance Policy

Policy Series: UL-800 | Product Type: Flexible-Premium Permanent Life Insurance

1. Overview

SecureFuture Universal Life Insurance provides lifelong coverage with flexible premium payments and an adjustable death benefit. A portion of each premium is credited to a cash value account that earns interest at a rate declared periodically by the insurer, subject to a guaranteed minimum rate.

2. Eligibility

Applicants must be between 18 and 80 years of age. Coverage amounts start at $50,000 with no stated maximum, subject to financial and medical underwriting. Applicants in the "Substandard" or "High Risk" underwriting tier are eligible with a rated premium multiplier applied to the cost of insurance charge.

3. Coverage Details

The policyholder may choose between a level death benefit (Option A) or a death benefit that increases by the accumulated cash value (Option B). Premiums, within limits, and the death benefit amount can be adjusted after issuance, subject to evidence of insurability for benefit increases.

4. Premium Flexibility

Unlike whole life, premium payments are not fixed. The policyholder may pay more than the minimum premium to build cash value faster, or skip payments if the existing cash value is sufficient to cover the monthly cost of insurance and administrative charges. If cash value is depleted, the policy may lapse unless additional premium is paid.

5. Exclusions

  • Suicide within the first 24 months of policy issuance or within 24 months of a death benefit increase.
  • Material misrepresentation of health or financial information during the two-year contestability period.

6. Cost of Insurance Charges

Each month, a cost of insurance charge is deducted from the cash value based on the insured's attained age, health classification, and net amount at risk. As the insured ages, this charge increases, which can erode cash value if premiums are not adjusted accordingly.

7. Claims Process

Beneficiaries must submit a claim form, certified death certificate, and the policy's certificate number. If death occurs during the contestability period, the insurer may conduct a full underwriting review of the original application before settling the claim.

8. Definitions

Cost of Insurance: The monthly charge deducted from the policy's cash value to pay for the pure insurance protection.

Net Amount at Risk: The death benefit minus the accumulated cash value, representing the insurer's actual insurance exposure.
