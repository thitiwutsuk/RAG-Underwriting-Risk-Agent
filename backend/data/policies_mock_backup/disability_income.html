SecureFuture Disability Income Insurance

Policy Series: DI-600 | Product Type: Individual Income Replacement Insurance

1. Overview

This policy replaces a portion of the insured's income if they become unable to work due to a covered illness or injury, paying a monthly benefit for the duration of the disability, up to the maximum benefit period elected.

2. Eligibility

Applicants must be between 18 and 60 years of age, employed at least 30 hours per week at application. The monthly benefit is limited to 60% of gross monthly income, to a maximum of $10,000/month. Applicants in occupation risk Class 4 or Class 5 are subject to a maximum benefit period of 5 years rather than to-age-65.

3. Elimination Period

Benefits begin after an elimination (waiting) period of 90 days of continuous disability, during which no benefit is payable. Shorter elimination periods of 30 or 60 days are available at application for an increased premium.

4. Definition of Disability

For the first 24 months of a claim, "disability" means the insured cannot perform the material duties of their own occupation. After 24 months, the definition changes to the inability to perform the duties of any occupation for which the insured is reasonably suited by education, training, or experience.

5. Exclusions

  • Disability resulting from self-inflicted injury.
  • Disability arising during the commission of an illegal act.
  • Normal pregnancy (complications of pregnancy are covered as any other illness).
  • Disability due to a pre-existing condition not disclosed at application, if it manifests within the first 12 months of coverage.

6. Partial and Residual Disability

If the insured returns to work part-time or at reduced income due to the same disabling condition, a proportional residual benefit is payable based on the percentage of income actually lost, provided the loss is at least 20% of prior income.

7. Claims Process

Claimants must submit a completed disability claim form, attending physician statement, and proof of income (e.g., pay stubs or tax returns) covering the 12 months prior to disability. The insurer may require periodic recertification of continued disability.

8. Definitions

Elimination Period: The waiting period between the onset of disability and the start of benefit payments.

Own Occupation: The specific occupation the insured was performing immediately before the disability began.
