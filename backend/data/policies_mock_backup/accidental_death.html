SecureFuture Accidental Death & Dismemberment (AD&D) Policy

Policy Series: AD-500 | Product Type: Accident-Only Insurance

1. Overview

This policy pays a benefit if the insured dies or suffers a specified bodily injury (such as loss of a limb or eyesight) solely as a direct result of an accident, independent of any illness or pre-existing condition.

2. Eligibility

Applicants must be between 18 and 70 years of age. This policy does not require a medical examination and is available regardless of health-based underwriting risk tier, since coverage responds only to accidental causes.

3. Coverage Details

Covered LossBenefit Payable
Accidental death100% of principal sum
Loss of both hands, both feet, or sight in both eyes100% of principal sum
Loss of one hand and one foot100% of principal sum
Loss of one hand, one foot, or sight in one eye50% of principal sum
Loss of thumb and index finger of the same hand25% of principal sum

4. Exclusions

  • Death or injury caused by illness, disease, or natural bodily degeneration.
  • Self-inflicted injury or suicide.
  • Participation in a hazardous hobby not declared and rated at application (e.g., skydiving, motor racing) unless a hazardous activity rider is in force.
  • Injury sustained while under the influence of alcohol or unprescribed drugs.
  • War or active military combat duty.

5. Time Limit for Loss

The covered loss must occur within 365 days of the accident that caused it for the benefit to be payable.

6. Claims Process

Claims must be filed within 90 days of the accident, accompanied by a police report (where applicable), medical records documenting the injury, and a completed claim form.

7. Definitions

Principal Sum: The maximum benefit amount elected at policy issuance.

Accident: A sudden, unexpected, and external event causing bodily injury, independent of illness or disease.
