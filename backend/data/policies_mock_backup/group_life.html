SecureFuture Group Life Insurance Policy

Policy Series: GL-700 | Product Type: Employer-Sponsored Group Term Life Insurance

1. Overview

This master policy provides term life insurance coverage to eligible employees of a participating employer group. Individual employees are covered under a Certificate of Insurance issued under this master policy, without individual medical underwriting up to the Guaranteed Issue amount.

2. Eligibility

Employees who are actively at work at least 20 hours per week and have completed any employer-specified waiting period (typically 30 to 90 days) are eligible. Coverage amounts are either a flat amount or a multiple of annual salary (e.g., 1x, 2x, or 3x salary), as elected by the employer.

3. Guaranteed Issue Amount

Coverage up to the Guaranteed Issue (GI) limit specified in the group policy schedule is provided without individual health questions or an underwriting risk tier assessment. Amounts elected above the GI limit require Evidence of Insurability and are subject to individual underwriting, including the standard age, BMI, health history, and occupation risk factors.

4. Coverage Details

The death benefit is payable to the employee's named beneficiary upon death from any cause while actively employed and covered under the group policy, subject to the exclusions below. Coverage generally terminates upon termination of employment, though a conversion privilege to an individual whole life policy is available within 31 days of termination.

5. Exclusions

  • Suicide within the first 12 months of an individually underwritten amount above the Guaranteed Issue limit.
  • War or active military combat duty, unless covered by employer rider.
  • Material misrepresentation on an Evidence of Insurability form.

6. Portability and Conversion

Upon leaving the employer, a covered employee may convert some or all of their group coverage to an individual whole life policy without evidence of insurability, provided the application for conversion is submitted within 31 days of coverage termination.

7. Claims Process

The employer's HR or benefits administrator typically assists the beneficiary with the claims process, which requires a certificate of insurance, a claim form, and a certified death certificate submitted to the group insurer.

8. Definitions

Master Policy: The group insurance contract held by the employer.

Certificate of Insurance: The document issued to each covered employee summarizing their coverage under the master policy.

Evidence of Insurability: A health questionnaire (and possible medical exam) required for coverage amounts above the Guaranteed Issue limit.
