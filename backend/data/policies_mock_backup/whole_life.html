SecureFuture Whole Life Insurance Policy

Policy Series: WL-200 | Product Type: Permanent Life Insurance with Cash Value

1. Overview

SecureFuture Whole Life Insurance provides lifelong coverage as long as premiums are paid, combined with a guaranteed cash value component that grows on a tax-deferred basis. Unlike term insurance, this policy does not expire at the end of a fixed period.

2. Eligibility

Applicants must be between 18 and 75 years of age at the time of application. Coverage amounts range from $25,000 to $1,000,000. Applicants in the "High Risk" underwriting tier may still qualify with a premium loading and a reduced maximum coverage amount of $250,000.

3. Coverage Details

The policy pays the full face amount to the beneficiary upon the insured's death, at any age, provided the policy remains in force. The policy also accumulates a cash surrender value, which the policyholder may borrow against or withdraw, subject to applicable loan interest and reduction of the death benefit.

4. Premium Payment Terms

Premiums are fixed for the life of the policy and do not increase with age. Premiums are determined at issue based on age, health classification, and coverage amount. A portion of each premium payment is allocated to the policy's cash value.

5. Exclusions

  • Suicide within the first 24 months of policy issuance.
  • Material misrepresentation of health history during the two-year contestability period.
  • Death resulting from participation in an illegal act.

6. Waiting Period

A waiting period of 24 months applies to the full death benefit for applicants issued under the "Substandard" or "High Risk" underwriting tiers with a declared chronic condition. During this period, the benefit payable for death from natural causes is limited to a return of premiums paid plus 2% interest.

7. Policy Loans and Withdrawals

After the policy has accumulated sufficient cash value (typically after year 3), the policyholder may take a loan against the cash value at the guaranteed policy loan interest rate. Outstanding loans and accrued interest reduce the death benefit payable to the beneficiary.

8. Claims Process

Beneficiaries must submit a claim form, certified death certificate, and proof of identity. Claims involving death within the contestability period may require additional investigation and can take up to 60 business days to process.

9. Definitions

Cash Value: The savings component of the policy that accumulates over time and can be borrowed against or withdrawn.

Face Amount: The death benefit amount stated in the policy.

Lapse: Termination of the policy due to non-payment of premiums, after which coverage ends unless the cash value is sufficient to cover premiums automatically.
