SecureFuture Term Life Insurance Policy

Policy Series: TL-100 | Product Type: Renewable and Convertible Term Life Insurance

1. Overview

SecureFuture Term Life Insurance provides a guaranteed death benefit to your named beneficiaries if you pass away during the policy term. This policy does not accumulate cash value and is designed to provide affordable, temporary protection for a fixed period of 10, 15, 20, or 30 years.

2. Eligibility

Applicants must be between 18 and 65 years of age at the time of application. Coverage amounts range from $50,000 to $2,000,000, subject to underwriting approval. Applicants classified as "Decline" under the current underwriting risk tier are not eligible for standard issue and must be referred to manual underwriting.

3. Coverage Details

Upon the death of the insured during the policy term, the insurer will pay the elected death benefit amount to the named beneficiary, provided all premiums have been paid and the policy is in force. The death benefit is payable regardless of the cause of death, subject to the exclusions listed in Section 5.

4. Premium Payment Terms

Premiums are calculated based on the applicant's age, sex, health classification (Preferred, Standard, Substandard, or High Risk), tobacco use, and coverage amount. Premiums are level for the initial term period. Substandard and High Risk classifications carry a premium loading of +50% to +150% over the Standard rate, as determined by the underwriting risk tier.

5. Exclusions

  • Suicide within the first 24 months of policy issuance (premiums paid will be refunded to the beneficiary).
  • Death resulting from participation in an illegal act.
  • Material misrepresentation of health history on the application (may result in claim denial or policy rescission within the two-year contestability period).
  • Death occurring during active military combat duty, unless a war rider has been purchased.

6. Waiting Period

There is no waiting period for death by accident. For death by natural causes, the full benefit is payable from the policy effective date, subject to the contestability period described in Section 5.

7. Renewability and Conversion

This policy is renewable at the end of the term without evidence of insurability, at a premium reflecting the insured's attained age. The policy may also be converted to a permanent whole life policy before the earlier of the end of the conversion period or the insured's 70th birthday, without a new medical exam.

8. Claims Process

To file a claim, the beneficiary must submit a completed claim form, a certified copy of the death certificate, and the original policy document (if available) to the claims department. Claims are typically processed within 30 business days of receipt of complete documentation.

9. Definitions

Insured: The individual whose life is covered by this policy.

Beneficiary: The person(s) or entity designated to receive the death benefit.

Contestability Period: The first two years after policy issuance, during which the insurer may investigate and contest a claim based on misrepresentation.
