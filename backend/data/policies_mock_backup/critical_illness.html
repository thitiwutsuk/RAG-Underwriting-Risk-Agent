SecureFuture Critical Illness Coverage

Policy Series: CI-300 | Product Type: Lump-Sum Critical Illness Benefit Rider or Standalone Policy

1. Overview

This policy pays a single lump-sum benefit upon initial diagnosis of a covered critical illness, regardless of actual medical expenses incurred. The benefit can be used for treatment costs, income replacement, or any other purpose.

2. Eligibility

Applicants must be between 18 and 60 years of age. Coverage amounts range from $10,000 to $500,000. Applicants with a prior diagnosis of cancer, heart disease, or stroke are not eligible for coverage of that specific condition (pre-existing condition exclusion applies for 12 months on other conditions).

3. Covered Conditions

  • Heart attack (myocardial infarction), as evidenced by specific diagnostic criteria.
  • Stroke resulting in permanent neurological deficit.
  • Invasive cancer, excluding early-stage or non-invasive cancers (a reduced 25% benefit applies to early-stage cancer).
  • Coronary artery bypass surgery.
  • Kidney failure requiring regular dialysis.
  • Major organ transplant.

4. Survival Period

The insured must survive at least 30 days following the diagnosis of a covered condition for the benefit to be payable. If death occurs within this survival period, no critical illness benefit is paid, though a separate life insurance benefit may apply if held.

5. Waiting Period

A 90-day waiting period applies from the policy effective date. Diagnoses made during this waiting period are not covered, except for critical illness resulting directly from a covered accidental injury.

6. Exclusions

  • Self-inflicted injury.
  • Illness or condition diagnosed outside the survival and waiting periods described above.
  • Conditions related to alcohol or drug abuse.
  • Non-disclosure of a pre-existing condition known to the applicant at the time of application.

7. Claims Process

Claimants must submit medical records confirming diagnosis, a completed claims form, and physician certification. The insurer may require an independent medical examination before approving the claim.

8. Definitions

Lump-Sum Benefit: A one-time payment made upon a qualifying diagnosis.

Pre-Existing Condition: Any condition for which the applicant received treatment, medication, or diagnosis within 24 months prior to the application date.
